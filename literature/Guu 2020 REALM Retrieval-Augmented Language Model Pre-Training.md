## In one sentence

Trains the retriever and language model jointly during pre-training by treating retrieved documents as latent variables.

---

## Key ideas

1. Retrieval is incorporated at pre-training time, not just inference
2. Uses MIPS (Maximum Inner Product Search) over a corpus as a differentiable memory
3. Shows that grounding LM pre-training in retrieval improves open-domain QA significantly
