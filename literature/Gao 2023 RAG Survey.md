## In one sentence

The most comprehensive RAG survey: taxonomizes Naive RAG → Advanced RAG → Modular RAG, covering retrieval, generation, augmentation, and evaluation.

---

## Key ideas

1. Three paradigms: Naive (retrieve-then-read), Advanced (rerank, compression, HyDE), Modular (flexible pipeline DAGs)
2. Covers the full stack: indexing, retrieval, reranking, context compression, generation
3. RAG vs. fine-tuning: RAG wins on dynamic knowledge; FT wins on style/format
4. Identifies open problems: multi-hop, long-context, retrieval granularity
