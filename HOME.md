## System

| Folder | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| `fleeting/` | Raw captures — process within 48h |
| `literature/` | Notes on books, articles, videos |
| `permanent/` | Atomic, evergreen ideas in your own words |
| `index/` | Maps of Content — clusters of notes |
| `templates/` | Blank templates to copy |

### Note ID format
`YYYYMMDD`HHmm — e.g. `202606121430` for June 12, 2026 at 14:30

### Naming convention
- Permanent: `202606121430 Writing is thinking made visible.md`
- Literature: `Ahrens 2017 How to Take Smart Notes.md`
- MOC: `MOC Programming.md`
- Fleeting: `202606121430 fleeting.md`
